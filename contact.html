Scholarly & Pedagogical Profile

Higher education biology educator and research biochemist with over 10 years of teaching experience. Specializes in Discipline-Based Education Research (DBER), transforming undergraduate classrooms into continuous Kaizen learning laboratories. Investigates how learners iteratively construct, refine, and apply scientific reasoning in unfamiliar biological contexts, with focal emphasis on student AI literacy, conceptual change, and evidence-informed curriculum redesign aligned with National Research Council (NRC, 2012) standards.

Higher Education & Teaching Leadership (10+ Years)

  • Undergraduate & Graduate Biology Instruction: Over a decade of teaching biochemistry, molecular biology, and general biology across diverse student cohorts.
  • DBER Curriculum Innovation: Designed inquiry-driven, problem-based learning modules emphasizing systems thinking, mechanism-building, and data evaluation.
  • AI Literacy & Scientific Reasoning: Lead author on pedagogical interventions investigating student interactions with generative AI in biology problem-solving.
  • Pedagogical Mentorship: Mentored teaching assistants and junior instructors on reflective teaching, continuous feedback loops, and inclusive classroom design.
